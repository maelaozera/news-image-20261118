One 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of **Wednesday 18 November 2026**,
built for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118).

The front page covers six stories from the day:

1. **Gulf on edge** — Iran drones target a US radar in Bahrain; the US hits Qeshm island; Kuwait's defences fire back.
2. **Trump in Manila** — Quad leaders meet in Manila as Trump heads on to China.
3. **Ukraine strikes** — the Crimean bridge is hit again as Russia pounds Kyiv's bridges.
4. **Cyclone Akvan** — the storm builds over the Bay of Bengal, severe by Thursday.
5. **Nvidia probe** — the chip giant hires a law firm over CEO spy-link claims.
6. **Dhoni bows out** — MS Dhoni ends his IPL playing career and becomes Chennai's mentor.

Also noted in the footer: Britain sanctions Israeli settlement firms, allies urge
Israel to open Gaza aid routes, and the EU weighs a €45bn loan for Ukraine.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. It needs no network, and every run writes the same bytes.
